# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-03

This Privacy Policy applies to `Poker AI Trainer` (`德扑AI训练器`).

## 1. Overview

Poker AI Trainer is a single-player poker training app. The app uses virtual training points only. It does not offer real-money gambling, cash betting, deposits, withdrawals, or cash redemption.

We aim to keep the product lightweight and privacy-conscious. Most gameplay data is stored locally on the device. Some analytics and advertising-related data may be processed by third-party service providers described below.

## 2. Information We Process

### Information stored locally on your device

The app stores the following on your device so core features can work:
- game progress and continue-game state
- recent hand history and statistics
- AI learning / profile data
- virtual training point balance
- welfare / reward claim state

### Analytics data

The app may use analytics services to understand product usage and improve stability and gameplay experience. Examples may include:
- app launches
- game starts and endings
- hand outcomes
- in-game actions
- session timing and tutorial completion

### Advertising-related data

If you choose to watch rewarded ads, the app may use advertising SDKs and consent tools to load and show ads. These services may process device- or ad-related data according to their own policies.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as:
- Firebase Analytics
- Google AdMob
- Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

These providers may process certain technical, usage, or advertising-related data under their own privacy terms.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information to:
- run the game and restore progress
- keep statistics and AI training records
- provide optional rewarded ads
- analyze product usage and improve the app
- comply with legal and platform obligations

## 5. Tracking and Personalized Advertising

The app is not designed as a social or real-money gambling product.

Where required by law, consent for advertising-related processing is requested through a consent flow before ads are requested. Availability and behavior of ads may vary by region and user consent status.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited data with service providers only to the extent needed to:
- deliver analytics
- serve rewarded ads
- support consent management

## 7. Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear in-app data where supported.

Third-party service providers may retain data according to their own retention policies.

## 8. Children

This app is not directed to children for real-money gambling purposes. If a region requires parental controls or age restrictions for simulated gambling content, the app store age rating and local platform controls apply.

## 9. Your Choices

Depending on your region and platform, you may have choices relating to:
- ad consent
- system-level privacy controls
- deleting the app and its local data
