## Poker AI Trainer Support

Thank you for using Poker AI Trainer.

Poker AI Trainer is an offline Texas Hold'em training app focused on virtual-chip practice against AI opponents. If you run into issues or have product questions, use the contact email below.

### Support Email

`billxfan@gmail.com`

### Common Topics

- Continue game is unavailable
- Rewarded ad did not appear
- Rewarded ad showed but reward was not credited
- Language display issue
- Statistics or recent hands look incorrect
- App review / privacy question

### What to include in a support email

- device model
- iOS version
- app version
- what you were doing before the issue happened
- screenshot or screen recording if available

### Important Product Note

Poker AI Trainer is for training and entertainment only.
- no real-money gambling
- no deposits or withdrawals
- all chips are virtual training points
