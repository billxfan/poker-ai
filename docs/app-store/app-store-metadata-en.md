## Description

Practice Texas Hold'em without real-money pressure.

Poker AI Trainer is a solo offline training app built for players who want to sharpen decision-making, table awareness, and adaptation against different opponent styles.

What you can do:
- Play six-handed no-limit Hold'em against five AI opponents
- Face distinct AI styles, from tight-aggressive to loose-passive
- Continue saved games and review your recent hands
- Check long-term results, profit trends, and AI reads on your play
- Earn virtual training points through daily rewards and optional rewarded ads

Why it is useful:
- No real-money betting
- No multiplayer pressure
- Fast repetition for pre-flop, post-flop, and showdown practice
- Clear feedback through hand history and statistics

Important notice:
- This app is for training and entertainment only
- No cash betting, no withdrawals, and no real-money gambling features
- All chips are virtual training points only
