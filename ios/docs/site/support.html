Support

Poker AI Trainer is an offline Texas Hold'em training app focused on virtual-chip practice against AI opponents. If you run into issues with rewards, gameplay, language display, or statistics, contact support below.

billxfan@gmail.com

Common Topics

  • Continue game is unavailable
  • Rewarded ad did not appear
  • Rewarded ad showed but reward was not credited
  • Language display issue
  • Statistics or recent hands look incorrect
  • Privacy or review-related questions

What to Include

  • device model
  • iOS version
  • app version
  • what happened before the issue
  • screenshot or screen recording if available

Important Product Note

  • single-player training only
  • no real-money gambling
  • no deposits or withdrawals
  • all chips are virtual training points
